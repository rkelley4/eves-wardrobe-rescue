Step by step

How it works

Five steps. You do two of them.

1

Message me

Tell me roughly what you've got and send a few photos. I'll tell you honestly what's likely to sell and what isn't, before you post anything.

→
2

Check it against the label

Have a proper look at each item in daylight, using the checklist below. This is the step that decides whether your items sell for a good price or sit there.

3

Post it to me

I'll send you the address privately once we've agreed. Pop in a note saying which charity you'd like your 20% to go to.

4

I do the boring bit

Photographing, measuring, writing descriptions, pricing, answering questions, haggling politely, packing and posting. I set prices based on my successful previous sales. This is the part that takes hours, and it's the part you're paying me 30% for.

5

You get paid

Within five days of the money clearing on Vinted, I send you your 50% and send your charity its 20%. I'll show you the sale so you can see the price for yourself.

Read this before you post

What I can and can't sell.

Vinted buyers leave honest reviews. One item with a stain on it can damage the rating that every other seller on this page depends on — so please check carefully. If you're not sure, send a photo and ask.

What I take: Clothes, shoes, bags, belts, scarves, jewellery, toys, and home items. I don't currently take beauty products or furniture, since they need extra care to sell safely and I want to do a great job with what I do take.

EVE'S WARDROBE RESCUE — QUALITY CHECK — PLEASE READ BEFORE POSTING

PLEASE SEND

  • Clean and freshly washed
  • All buttons, zips and clasps working
  • Shape still good, no sagging
  • Colour still strong
  • Brand label still readable
  • Shoes with no major sole wear
  • Bags with clean linings, no rips
  • Jewellery in working condition (clasps, chains intact)
  • Toys with all original pieces and parts
  • Battery toys tested and working
  • Home items clean, with no cracks or damage
  • Anything you'd happily buy yourself

PLEASE DON'T SEND

  • Stains, marks or discolouring
  • Holes, rips or unravelling seams
  • Broken zips or missing buttons
  • Bobbling or pilling
  • Stretched cuffs or necklines
  • Smoke, damp or perfume smells
  • Faded or heavily washed-out colour
  • Counterfeit or fake designer items
  • Shoes with worn-down soles or heels
  • Bags with broken zips or stained linings
  • Costume jewellery that's tarnished or missing stones
  • Toys missing pieces or parts
  • Recalled toys, or anything with a safety warning
  • Cracked, chipped, or broken home items

ANYTHING THAT ARRIVES IN UNSELLABLE CONDITION WILL BE PASSED TO A TEXTILE RECYCLING BANK RATHER THAN LISTED. I'LL ALWAYS TELL YOU IF THIS HAPPENS.

You choose

Pick where your 20% goes.

Every seller chooses their cause when they send their parcel. Three options, because more than three makes it hard to decide.

Option one

Little Lives UK

Donates refurbished laptops, tablets and computers to UK schools and families who can't otherwise afford them. Registered charity 1171884 .

Option two

Tech She Can

Tech She Can is a registered UK charity (no. 1195284) whose mission specifically includes educating and inspiring girls and women to pursue technology careers. It works with children from age 5 upwards through its Tech We Can resources and also develops pathways into technology careers later on.

Option three

The Toy Project

Collects toys all year round and gives them to children in need across the UK — not just at Christmas, so every sale can support them any time of year. Not a registered charity, but an established community organisation — you can see their work at thetoyproject.co.uk.

After 60 days

What if something doesn't sell?

Some things just don't find a buyer, however good they are. If an item is still listed after 60 days, I'll message you and you choose what happens next. Nothing gets thrown away, and I never decide for you.

A

Send it back to me

I'll pack it up and post it home. You cover the return postage, and I'll tell you exactly what it costs before I send it.

B

Donate it to charity

I'll take it to a charity shop and send you a photo of the donation. Free, and the items still do some good.

If I don't hear back within 14 days of asking, I'll go with option B and let you know.
